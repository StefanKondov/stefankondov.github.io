School for Work — UZR 2026

Team lead
Team Lead — "Училище за работа" (School for Work)
Give Time Foundation · 2026
UZR is a 6-day intensive career programme bringing together 40 young people without parental care from across Bulgaria. They travel to Sofia and are split into eight professional teams — restaurants, bakeries, hospitality, construction, photography, beauty studios, IT, and advertising agencies — working shoulder to shoulder with professionals and volunteer mentors.

This year Stefan stepped up as a team lead for one of the eight squads, guiding young participants through real workplace experiences, helping them build confidence, work habits, and communication skills — and supporting them through every step from writing their first CV to presenting at the final showcase fair.
